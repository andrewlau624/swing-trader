# ETF twins for the IBS leg (2026-10-08)

Verdict: twins help, but by little (about +0.5 to +1.7pp/yr of account equity at $2-10k). Not implemented: the gain is small and the wash-guard and noise-leg interactions are not a safe config-only change. No branch created; tree is on main.

## Live IBS mechanics (code facts)
- Universe `ibs_symbols` (config.yaml:94): SPY QQQ IWM DIA MDY XLK XLF XLE XLV XLI XLY XLP XLU XLB SMH XBI EEM EFA. `ibs_top_k: 3` ranks them by 12-1 momentum each month (`signals.momentum_top`). Names with IBS < 0.2 on the last bar are held. Today's top-3 is SMH, XBI, XLE.
- Sizing (`executor._ibs_open`): per = `ibs_weight` (0.5) x equity / n_targets. Schwab broker does `floor(notional/ref_px)` (`brokers.py:333`). Leftover stays cash (SGOV is bought only when no targets).
- Existing twin machinery, each for wash/stand-in reasons only, not for rounding:
  - `signals.WASH_LOOKALIKE` / `WASH_SAFE_LOOKALIKE`: the Roth trades a different-index look-alike (XLE->VDE, SMH->SOXX, XLK->VGT, ...). `WASH_SAME_INDEX` = SPY QQQ IWM MDY EEM: the Roth does not use the look-alike and skips the name if the taxable book traded it in 31 days. DIA and XBI have none.
  - `noise_alt_symbol` QQQM and `noise_extra {SMH: SOXX}`: the taxable noise leg swaps symbols on days the IBS leg holds the original.

## 3. Why not implemented
- **Same-index twins break the wash guard.** SPY->SPYM, QQQ->QQQM, MDY->IJH, IWM->VTWO and EEM->IEMG are substantially identical across accounts. The guard compares exact symbols, so the Roth would still buy SPY while the taxable account holds or sold SPYM. Doing this safely means rewriting `_wash_symbols`, `_roth_ibs_targets`, `signals.wash_g4s` and the G4s shadow.
- **SMH->SOXQ is the only different-index twin that matters** (the SMH slot is 76% deployed at $818). It still collides with the noise leg's SMH->SOXX stand-in and with the Roth's SMH->SOXX, because held-position keying, exits and `foreign` filtering in `_ibs_open` are all by exact symbol. A target-to-exec symbol map there, plus tests on the wash and noise paths, touches live money paths for about 1pp/yr.
- Cheaper alternatives, user decisions:
  - (a) Roth only: replace the Roth SMH look-alike SOXX with SOXQ (a one-line change in `WASH_LOOKALIKE`; not same-index, so wash-safe vs the taxable SMH; fixes the 0-share rejection). Needs the same check that SOXQ is not held by another leg or the noise stand-in.
  - (b) Let the leftover cash (about 8% taxable, 38% Roth) go to SGOV instead of idling; about 4%/yr on the leftover, trivial.
  - (c) Accept it: above about $10k the loss is under 0.5pp.
