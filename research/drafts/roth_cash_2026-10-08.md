## 3. GFV analysis (cash IRA, T+1 settlement)

Rule (Schwab): a good-faith violation = buy with unsettled sale proceeds, then SELL that security before the funding sale settles (T+1). A sale made on day d settles at the start of d+1, so selling on d+1 at any time is fine; selling on d (same day) is the violation.

Timelines (d = a trading day; funding sale S, bought security B, B's sale):
- **Night leg (close -> open).** Open d: night positions sold (S), proceeds unsettled until d+1. Close d: MOC buy of B funded by S or settled cash. Open d+1: B sold. S settled at start of d+1, so B is sold on the settlement date: no GFV. Friday: S settles Monday, B sold Monday open: same, no GFV. Holiday: same logic on the next business day.
- **IBS leg (open -> open).** Open d (09:16 submit, fills ~09:50): sell SGOV (S) and/or night names, buy IBS ETF B with the unsettled proceeds. B sold at open d+2 (or later). S settled d+1: no GFV. The intermediate overnight close-d MOC night buy is funded by the same unsettled cash but is a different security sold d+1: no GFV.
- **SGOV parking sales.** SGOV bought at 09:16-09:50 day d, funded by unsettled proceeds, is sold at the earliest on the next IBS signal day >= d+1: no GFV. SGOV bought d is never sold d. A GFV needs a SAME-DAY round trip or selling a security within T+1 of buying it with unsettled funds when the funding sale's settlement is AFTER the sale date. Case that does break it: SGOV bought on d with unsettled proceeds of a d sale (settles d+1) and SGOV sold on d (e.g. an IBS signal appearing intraday or a missed open fill retried the same afternoon at 15:57). Not in the current schedule.
- **The only schedule item that is a textbook GFV in a cash account is the 3x-ETF intraday leg** (buy 10:01+ with the morning's unsettled proceeds, flatten 15:57 the same day). It runs because limited margin (R10) lets unsettled funds trade. Also `kind='-day'` retry sells (executor.py ~1578) and the safety-net open flatten are sells of securities bought earlier, fine.

**Verdict:** the night and IBS legs and T-bill parking are GFV-safe even WITHOUT limited margin, given the funding sale settles the morning the next sale occurs. Only the intraday 3x leg needs limited margin. Consequently nothing in the current schedule would be unsafe if the idle cash were deployed more fully, with one caveat: buying power. Schwab does not credit unexecuted sells, so a 09:16 buy that depends on a not-yet-filled 09:30 sale can be rejected (RESULTS.md Q4 item 1); the Roth's idle cash covers it at $1k.
