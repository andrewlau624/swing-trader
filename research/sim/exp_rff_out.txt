=== Implementation choices (spec ambiguities, conservative / stated before reading any judge number) ===
 - Data: Sharadar SFP 'close' here is split-adjusted but NOT dividend-adjusted (close == closeunadj after the last split); a total-return close was built by adding cache_ACTIONS dividends on the ex-date (dividend assumed split-adjusted), O/H/L scaled by the same factor. Alpaca etf_daily is split+dividend-adjusted; 2021+ is chained from its close-to-close returns.
 - FIT nominally starts 2002-08; the first row with all 400 base features defined is ~140 sessions after TLT's 2002-07-30 listing.
 - 'max drawdown from window high' = log(close at window end / max close in window). 'mean IBS' = mean over the L+1 sessions [t-o-L, t-o]; realised vol = std (ddof 0) of the L daily log returns in the window.
 - Ridge: unpenalised intercept (training-mean centering of features and labels), penalty lambda*||b||^2, kernel form. f = forecast minus the training-label mean (the timing signal around 1x); sd(f_fit) = sd of walk-forward out-of-sample f over 2009-01..2010-12 (refit every 21 sessions, inside the fit period). A2 ranks the full forecast (intercept + f).
 - Lambda: one split, train = rows whose target is realised by 2008-12-31, validate = 2009-01..2010-12 rows (target realised by 2010-12-31), minimum validation MSE (ties -> larger lambda); A2 picks lambda per ETF model the same way.
 - Walk-forward: refits at the first 2009 row and every 21 rows; a refit at row tau trains on rows < tau (last label realised at tau's close). Hyperparameters never change after the split.
 - Placebo: the label vector (all 8 ETFs together for A2) is circularly shifted by k~U{250..2000} over the whole training segment (rows whose target is realised by 2020-12-31), and the entire pipeline (lambda, sd(f_fit), walk-forward) is rerun; P&L uses true returns. 200 draws, rng seed 12345; seed-0 features. Gate 5 compares judge alpha with the placebo alphas.
 - Costs: c x |position change| per day (A2: sum of |weight changes|; intraday drift of weights ignored). P&L dated at t+1.
 - Gate 4 read as gate 1 at 3bp/side (alpha > 0 AND NW t >= 2). NW lags 5 on the HAC OLS of strategy net on benchmark.
 - Seeds: rng(seed) draws base features then the RFF matrix. A2 gated on seed 0 only (the 3-of-5 rule is stated for A1).
 - REPORT ends at the last Alpaca session on disk, not 2026-10.
